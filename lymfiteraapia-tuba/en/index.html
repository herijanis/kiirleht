Where does the swelling sit?

Lymph flows from the arms, legs and face towards the lymph nodes. When this flow slows down, swelling appears. Choose an area to see where therapy guides the lymph.

Frequently asked questions

Who is lymphatic therapy for?

For example, primary and secondary lymphoedema, swelling after cancer treatment, chronic venous insufficiency, lipoedema, swelling after injury or surgery, and swelling during pregnancy. I always assess suitability individually, taking your doctor's advice into account.

How do I get 90% off compression garments?

You need a digital medical device card issued by your doctor and must meet the reimbursement conditions. The reimbursement is based on the product's reference price; anything above it is paid by the buyer. I measure and order the garments on site.
