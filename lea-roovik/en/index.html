Frequently asked questions

Can my massage be covered?

Yes – through Stebby, SportID and health insurance. Just ask when you book.

Where exactly are you?

In the courtyard at Müürivahe 15, 2nd floor, near Freedom Square (Vabaduse väljak). Brown door, press buzzer no. 2.

When are you open?

Every day 9–21 by appointment. Book by text, email or phone.

Do you also come to homes or offices?

Yes, in Tallinn. Massage at home: 60 min 90 €, 90 min 100 €; at work: 30 min 35 €, 60 min 45 €. Events by agreement.

Can two of us come together?

Yes – a dual massage for two in one room takes 75 minutes and costs 140 €.

How do I buy a gift card?

Order right here on this page. Payment by bank transfer; the card is valid for 6 months and reaches the recipient as a PDF.
