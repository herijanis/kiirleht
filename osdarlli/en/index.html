Frequently asked questions

How does the first visit work?

On the first visit, treatments cost less (e.g. ICOONE 3 zones 70 €, cavitation 50 €). Then you can decide whether to continue with a course.

How many treatments do I need?

Courses range from 6 to 30 treatments. We'll recommend how many suit you at your first visit.

How long are packages valid?

Packages are valid for 2–4 months and can be frozen if needed.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes, packages can be paid in instalments with ESTO.

What if I can't make it?

Cancel at least 24 hours in advance. The no-show fee is 50 €.

Are there any contraindications?

Yes, as with any device-based treatment: pregnancy and breastfeeding, a pacemaker, oncological diseases, acute inflammation and skin damage in the treatment area. If in doubt, consult a doctor.

Which studio should I come to?

In Kristiine we're at Seebi 1, room 208 (tel. +372 5814 8088), in Nõmme at Vabaduse pst 13 (tel. +372 5862 6608). The 45 € body shaping price applies at the Seebi 1 studio.

Are the devices certified?

Yes – all our devices have certificates.

Do you speak Russian?

Yes, we speak Estonian and Russian.
