Massage course for beginners

Jüri founded Massaažikoolitus in 2008 to bring simple, effective massage to everyone. The basic course lasts two months and gives you a solid grounding in what massage is about.

A skill like learning to ride a bike – you can't forget it.

How the course has run before

The 2018 course had 12 evening sessions from 17:00 to 20:00: each time one hour of anatomy and three hours of practice. By the end you can massage the back, legs, arms and neck.

Courses run rarely and in small groups, so the focus stays on quality.

Jüri Mälkmasseur and trainer, owner of Massaažikoolitus OÜ

Jüri came to massage in 2003 through a Chinese Tuina massage course and has been massaging people ever since – in spas, sports clubs and his own practice in Tartu.

Working with athletes taught him what really helps: quickly, precisely and without too much talk.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a massage cost?

Classic massage (50 min) and Chinese Tuina massage (60 min) cost 50 €, sports and therapeutic massage (50 min) 60 €.

How do I book?

Call or text +372 5356 2776, use the form on this page or pick a free slot in the online calendar. You can cancel up to 1 hour before.

When is the next massage course?

Courses run rarely and in small groups. Email jyri@massaazikoolitus.ee and Jüri will let you know when the next group starts.

How much does the course cost and how long is it?

The classic massage course costs 500 € and lasts two months.

Can I buy a gift card?

Yes. You choose the amount – call or message.

Where does the massage take place?

In Tartu, at Marja 22-7.
