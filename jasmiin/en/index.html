Frequently asked questions

Where is the salon?

In the centre of Jõhvi, at Veski 1. Parking is free and the salon is wheelchair accessible.

How much do treatments cost?

A manicure with gel strengthening and colour is 45–50 €, volume lashes 2D–4D 55 €, brow shaping 40–99.95 € and hair colour with cut and blow-dry 150 € (depends on the products used). We'll tell you the price of other treatments when booking.

How do I book?

Pick a treatment and a free time on this page or call +372 332 0222. For SMS and WhatsApp use +372 5695 8345.

Can I cancel?

Yes, free of charge up to 24 hours before.

Can I pay by card?

Yes, card or cash.

Do you have gift cards?

Yes. Order one right here on the page or ask at the salon.

Come and visit
